Space Shooter

How to Play

Objective: Destroy incoming alien ships and bosses. Survive as long as possible or complete all levels.

Keyboard Controls:

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move player ship
  • Spacebar: Continuous Fire
  • P: Pause / Resume Game

Touch Controls:

  • Tap/Hold Left Half of Screen: Move Left
  • Tap/Hold Right Half of Screen: Move Right
  • Tap/Hold Bottom 20% of Screen: Continuous Fire

Endless Mode: Face continuous, increasingly difficult waves of aliens and bosses. Aim for the highest score!

Levels Mode: Progress through 20 distinct levels, each with 5 waves and a boss battle. Unlock new levels by defeating bosses.
